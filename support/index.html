恢复购买

如果您已经购买过 Subdeck Pro，可以在 App 内使用“恢复购买”恢复相应 Pro 权益。

请确保当前设备已登录用于购买的同一 Apple ID。

年度订阅

年度 Pro 通过 Apple App Store 提供，属于自动续期订阅。

订阅会按年自动续期，除非您在当前计费周期结束前取消。

您可以在 Apple ID 的订阅设置中管理或取消年度订阅。

终身购买

终身 Pro 为一次性购买，不属于自动续期订阅。

完成购买后，您可以持续使用相应的 Pro 权益，无需按年续费。

iCloud 同步

iCloud 同步用于在您使用同一 Apple ID 的设备之间同步相关数据。

该功能依赖您的 Apple ID、iCloud 状态以及网络环境。如果 iCloud 未登录、同步未开启，或网络不可用，同步可能无法完成。

通知提醒

Subdeck 的提醒由设备上的本地通知提供，用于订阅相关提醒。

您可以随时在 iOS 设置中管理 Subdeck 的通知权限。

退款

App Store 购买和退款由 Apple 处理。

Subdeck 不直接处理付款，也无法代替 Apple 完成退款。如需申请退款，请通过 Apple 提供的退款流程操作。

Subdeck Support

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

This page explains how to get support for Subdeck, as well as information about purchases, subscriptions, iCloud Sync, notifications, and related terms.

Contact

If you need help using Subdeck, you can contact us at:

Developer: QiHu Wang

Email: qihu.studio@gmail.com

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased Subdeck Pro, you can use Restore Purchases in the app to restore your Pro access.

Please make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Annual Subscription

Annual Pro is offered through the Apple App Store as an auto-renewable subscription.

The subscription renews automatically each year unless you cancel before the current billing period ends.

You can manage or cancel the annual subscription in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Lifetime Purchase

Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase and is not an auto-renewable subscription.

After purchase, you can continue using the relevant Pro features without yearly renewal.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is used to synchronize relevant data across devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

This feature depends on your Apple ID, iCloud status, and network conditions. Sync may not complete if iCloud is not signed in, sync is disabled, or the network is unavailable.

Notifications

Reminders in Subdeck are provided by local notifications on your device for subscription-related alerts.

You can manage Subdeck notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Refunds

App Store purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

Subdeck does not process payments directly and cannot issue refunds on Apple’s behalf. To request a refund, please use the refund process provided by Apple.
